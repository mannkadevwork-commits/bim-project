For walls, follow these rules:

  • Keep continuous walls together. Draw one wall run instead of many small pieces.
  • Split only at real openings. Doors and windows should create the split when the wall actually opens.
  • Keep thickness consistent. Wall pieces belonging to the same wall should have similar thickness.
  • Avoid tiny fragments. Don't create labels for small line artifacts or accidental pieces.
  • Don't draw through openings. A real door/window opening should not be labeled as wall.
  • At junctions, keep wall runs clean. Avoid unnecessary gaps, duplicates, or tiny overlaps. (No CAD-style junction tools yet.)

Tip: At a door, finish the wall at the jamb and continue on the other side of the opening. Do not split a wall just because the drawing line looks slightly broken — if the physical wall is continuous and the break is only a scan/drawing artifact, treat it as one wall run.

Goal: Make the label match the actual physical wall, not every small piece of visible ink.
